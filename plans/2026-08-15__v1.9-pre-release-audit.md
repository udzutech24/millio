## Decision

- Chosen approach: evidence ladder — baseline, static contracts, focused Backup/Restore tests, full automated gates, simulator flows, physical device, Release/archive, then read-only App Store checks.
- Rejected alternatives: relying on prior reports or a single green build; both leave data-loss and production-service risks unproven.
- Rollback strategy: no production mutation. Preserve the dirty worktree. Use isolated simulator/test stores; stop before any irreversible device or cloud action.
