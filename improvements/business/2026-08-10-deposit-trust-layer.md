# Deposit trust layer

Status: OPEN

## Fact

Millio already connects deposit schedule, account history, portfolio totals and Cashflow. A bank app normally shows only its own deposit, while Millio can show all deposits and their owner-wide tax/FX completeness in one place.

## Hypothesis

The defensible product advantage is not another rate calculator. It is a trust layer that clearly separates confirmed income, estimated due income, future income and incomplete tax/FX across all banks. This can reduce the user's need for a spreadsheet and make deposit maturity actionable.

## How to validate

Test a prototype with 5–7 users who hold deposits in two or more banks. Ask them to answer balance, earned income, next payout, maturity amount and required action in under 10 seconds. Compare the specialized hero against the current generic detail; measure answer accuracy and time, not visual preference.

## Decision trigger

Proceed with the full UX vertical only if the specialized prototype materially improves answer accuracy/time after core semantics are hardened.
