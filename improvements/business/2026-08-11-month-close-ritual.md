# Month close as a recurring retention ritual

## Fact

The user explicitly wants bank-statement upload to make it convenient to “close the month”, not merely to batch-create expenses.

## Hypothesis

A trustworthy review → resolve → close loop can create a monthly retention ritual and differentiate Millio from passive dashboards. The value is confidence that the month is complete, not the decorative status badge.

## How to test

- Measure workspace visits, statement/manual import completion, blocking checklist resolution, close completion and reopen rate without PII.
- Interview users who abandon after import review: unsupported bank, too many corrections, unclear account mapping or no value in closing.
- Success signal: repeat close behavior across 2–3 months and declining correction rate for known merchants.
