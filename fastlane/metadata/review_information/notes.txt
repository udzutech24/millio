millio is an offline-first personal finance app. No login is required to use the app — all data is stored locally on device.

To test the app:
1. Launch the app
2. Add an account (e.g. "My Card", type: debit, balance: 1000 USD)
3. Add a transaction to see cashflow tracking
4. The Backup feature requires iCloud to be enabled on the device

No special credentials are needed.
